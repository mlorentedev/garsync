# Capturing a Fitdays-only smart body-composition scale into a self-hosted pipeline

**Bottom line.** "Fitdays" is not a scale family, it is the app (Guangdong ICOMON, package `cn.fitdays.fitdays`) that fronts at least three physically different BLE scale families — and two of them broadcast or serve the measurement to *whoever is listening*, with no account binding at the link layer. So the requirement "readable only through the FitDays app" is a statement about the app's cloud, not about the hardware: a Linux host or an ESP32 in BLE range can capture weight and (on the right family) impedance without the phone, and the vendor's own body-composition numbers can be pulled in parallel from the undocumented Fitdays cloud with two MIT-licensed reverse-engineered clients. The honest ranking is: passive BLE (if your unit belongs to the broadcast family) ≈ active GATT daemon (if it belongs to the `FFB0` connect family) for real-time and vendor independence; cloud polling (`fitdays` on PyPI, ~30 min worst case) for the richest payload and 400 days of backfill; CSV/Health-app export only as a fallback. First step is not a decision, it is an identification: which of the three families your particular scale is in.

All findings were checked on **2026-09-23**. Markers: **MEASURED** = I fetched the cited source/document and read the statement in it; **CLAIMED** = a README, issue thread, doc page or forum asserts it and I did not independently reproduce it; **INFERRED** = my own reasoning over measured facts.

## Findings

### 1. Taxonomy — the premise conflates an app vendor, two OEMs and an unrelated watch SDK

1. **Fitdays is ICOMON's app; Welland and OKOK are OEMs that ship hardware into it.** — **MEASURED**: Welland's own product page for the FI2505LBF 8-electrode body-fat scale says it connects to "the Fitdays or Fitdays+ app" ([welland.hk FI2505LBF](https://www.welland.hk/products_details_en/FI2505LBF.html)). The `fitdays` client's own constants file documents the app as `cn.fitdays.fitdays` by GUANGDONG ICOMON ([constants.py](https://github.com/AboveColin/fitdays/blob/main/fitdays/constants.py)). OKOK International hardware is handled by openScale as a Chipsea-based family (`com.chipsea.btcontrol.en` is the companion app named in openScale issue #950, **CLAIMED**).
2. **FitCloudPro is a smartwatch SDK, not a scale protocol.** — **MEASURED**: `htangsmart/FitCloudPro-SDK-iOS` and `FitCloudPro-SDK-Android` are watch frameworks ("智能手表表盘开发套件", watch-face and DFU kits) ([FitCloudPro-SDK-iOS](https://github.com/htangsmart/FitCloudPro-SDK-iOS), [Codeberg mirror of the Android SDK](https://codeberg.org/vanous/FitCloudPro-SDK-Android)). I found **no** evidence of a body-composition scale using FitCloudPro. Treat any "FitCloudPro scale" statement as unverified.
3. **The `0xFFF0/0xFFF1/0xFFF2` vendor-service trio in the brief is not the Fitdays family.** — **MEASURED** against the two decoders I read: the connectable Fitdays/Icomon class uses `0xFFB0` (write `0xFFB1`, notify `0xFFB2`). `0xFFF0` appears in `ble-scale-sync` as Senssun *Model A* ([supported scales](https://blescalesync.dev/guide/supported-scales)). Do not start a reverse-engineering effort from the FFF0 assumption.

### 2. What the hardware actually exposes — three families

4. **Family A — passive advertisement broadcast ("AAA-series"): weight always, impedance present in the stream but undecoded upstream.** — **MEASURED**: `AAAxHandler.kt` in openScale master is `LinkMode.BROADCAST_ONLY` for device names `AAA002`/`AAA007`/`AAA013`. Frame: manufacturer data ≥12 bytes, bytes 6..11 XOR-deobfuscated with key `(companyId >> 8) & 0xFF`; checksum `sum(payload[0..4]) & 0x1F == payload[5] & 0x1F`; `payload[4] == 0xAD` is weight, a 32-bit big-endian value `[state:1 | reserved:13 | grams:18]` with `state == 1` meaning final; `payload[4] == 0xA6` is an **impedance frame which the handler logs and ignores** ("protocol known but not implemented here") ([AAAxHandler.kt](https://github.com/oliexdev/openScale/blob/master/android_app/app/src/main/java/com/health/openscale/core/bluetooth/scales/AAAxHandler.kt)). MEASURED-absence: an unmerged PR identical in intent — `IcomonBroadcastHandler`, "0xA0 XOR cipher" over `0xA0AC` manufacturer data yielding weight **and** impedance for newer Icomon boards that fail GATT with `UNKNOWN_STATUS_CODE` — is **not** in master ([PR #1345](https://github.com/oliexdev/openScale/pull/1345); the file 404s and the directory listing contains only `AAAxHandler.kt`). Note the cipher keys agree: `0xA0AC >> 8 == 0xA0`.
5. **Independent confirmation that a Fitdays-companion scale broadcasts weight and impedance in the advertisement.** — **CLAIMED**: an e-volve-branded scale whose companion app is Fitdays "is one you cannot connect to; weight and impedance information is simply broadcasted in the advertisement packets", opened specifically to get an ESP32/OpenMQTTGateway decoder written ([theengs/decoder#503](https://github.com/theengs/decoder/issues/503)). That request is still an open issue; I found no such decoder in the Theengs device table.
6. **Family B — OKOK/Chipsea broadcast: weight and raw impedance in the advertisement, body composition derived client-side.** — **MEASURED**: `OkOkHandler.kt` is `BROADCAST_ONLY`, matching device names `ADV`, `Chipsea-BLE`, `Yoda0*`, `Yoda1*`, or an unnamed device whose manufacturer-id low byte is `0xC0`. Manufacturer ids: `0x20CA` (V20), `0x11CA` (V11), `0xF0FF` (VF0). V20 is a 19-byte frame with an XOR checksum seeded at `0x20`, `finalFlag = data[6] & 0x01`, weight as u16 big-endian divided by 100 or 10 depending on `data[6] & 0x04`, and **impedance as u16 big-endian `/ 10.0f`**. V11 (23 bytes) and VF0 (≥4 bytes) are weight-only. On the `0xC0` path openScale computes water, visceral fat, body fat, muscle, LBM, bone, BMR, protein and metabolic age from weight+impedance via `OkOkV2Lib` ([OkOkHandler.kt](https://github.com/oliexdev/openScale/blob/master/android_app/app/src/main/java/com/health/openscale/core/bluetooth/scales/OkOkHandler.kt)).
7. **Family C — the connectable SWAN / Icomon / YG ("MGB") link, which can return the scale's own body composition.** — **MEASURED**: service `0xFFB0`, config write `0xFFB1`, notify `0xFFB2`. Session flow as implemented: enable notify, then write `F7 00 00 00`, `FA 00 00 00`, `FB <sex> <age> <height_cm>`, `FD <yy> <mm> <dd>`, `FC <HH> <MM> <SS>`, `FE 06 <unit> 00`. Two wire variants: **composite 20-byte** frames carrying weight + fat in part one and muscle + bone + water in part two, and **streaming 8-byte** frames `AC 02 … ` with `0xCE` = settling, `0xCA` = final weight, `0xCB` = impedance (`0xFD 0x00` opens the block, `0xFD 0xFF` = impedance unavailable). Body composition is completed client-side with `StandardImpedanceLib` ([MGBHandler.kt](https://github.com/oliexdev/openScale/blob/master/android_app/app/src/main/java/com/health/openscale/core/bluetooth/scales/MGBHandler.kt)).
8. **The same `FFB0` family is decoded independently in a second project, with the exact frame bytes.** — **CLAIMED** (contributor captures, internally consistent): stable weight `ac0203490000ca16` → `0x0349` = 841 → 84.1 kg; impedance `AC 02 FD 01 02 06 CB D1` → `0x0206` = **518 Ω**, reproducible across weigh-ins; `FB 02 1F A5` decodes as sex/age/height; `FE 06 <unit>` sets the display unit; **omitting the profile command still returned valid impedance** for that unit ([ble-scale-sync#322](https://github.com/KristianP26/ble-scale-sync/issues/322)). The Hutbit **218008 / WL292** adapter was decoded from two Android **Fitdays** HCI snoops and is listed as shipping ([ble-scale-sync#268](https://github.com/KristianP26/ble-scale-sync/issues/268)).
9. **No account binding at the link layer — but only one central at a time.** — **INFERRED** from MEASURED code: neither `MGBHandler` nor `OkOkHandler` performs any account, token or key exchange before the scale reports a measurement; the connectable flow is just "write config, step on, receive". The only binding in the system is at the app/cloud level (`bind_device`, device list). BLE itself permits one active connection, and vendor docs state "only one phone can be connected via Bluetooth at a time" (**CLAIMED**, [fitdays.org](https://fitdays.org/docs/caution-info/multiple-phones-connection)) — so a capture daemon must not run while the Fitdays app holds the link.
10. **Impedance is not on the ohm scale for every family, and one project says so explicitly.** — **MEASURED**: `ble-scale-sync` marks Active Era BS-06 as "reports a resistance, but its scaling has never been checked against a capture" and Trisa/ADE as "the resistance it reports is not on an ohm scale", estimating body fat from BMI instead ([supported scales](https://blescalesync.dev/guide/supported-scales)). Whichever route you take, validate the ohm value against a known-good figure before trusting derived fat%.

### 3. Open-source integrations — what actually supports this family

11. **openScale: yes for the OKOK/Chipsea broadcast family and the `FFB0` connect family; the broadcast family's impedance frame is currently dropped.** — **MEASURED** in the wiki and in source: wiki rows `OKOK V11/V20/VF0 → OkOkHandler, BLE Broadcast`; `AAA-series Broadcast Scale (AAA002) → AAAxHandler`; body-composition column is `n/a` for V11/VF0 and `o` (partial) for V20 ([openScale wiki](https://github.com/oliexdev/openScale/wiki/Supported-scales-in-openScale)). Android-only.
    - Licence **GPL-3.0**, **2,554 stars**, **526 forks**, `pushed_at 2026-09-22` (≈1 day before access), **not archived** — alive and actively maintained (GitHub API `repos/oliexdev/openScale`).
12. **`ble-scale-sync`: the strongest single project for this problem, and it already exports to Garmin Connect.** — **MEASURED**: supported-scale list includes **MGB (Swan / Icomon / YG) → "Yes" body composition**, **Hutbit 218008 / WL292 → Yes** ("also sold under stock SWAN branding"), **Robi S9 → Weight only**, and **Speediance FG2211WBF → Yes** ("Lefu/Icomon sibling of the Robi S9") ([supported scales](https://blescalesync.dev/guide/supported-scales)). 11 exporters including Garmin Connect ("Automatic body composition upload, no phone app needed"), MQTT (HA auto-discovery, 10 sensors), InfluxDB, Webhook, and local CSV/JSONL ([exporters](https://blescalesync.dev/exporters)). Transports: native BlueZ, Docker, an **ESP32 proxy that also relays GATT connects over MQTT**, an **ESPHome Bluetooth proxy** (Native API :6053), and **Home Assistant's advertisement stream** (advertisements only — no GATT) ([esp32-proxy](https://blescalesync.dev/guide/esp32-proxy), [esphome-proxy](https://blescalesync.dev/guide/esphome-proxy), [ha-bluetooth](https://blescalesync.dev/guide/ha-bluetooth)).
    - Licence **GPL-3.0**, **176 stars**, **44 forks**, created 2026-01-13, `pushed_at 2026-09-14`, release v1.12.0 dated 2026-05-06, **not archived**. TypeScript/Node; a Python 3.9+ sidecar is used for Garmin auth (GitHub API `repos/KristianP26/ble-scale-sync`; [releases](https://github.com/KristianP26/ble-scale-sync/releases/tag/v1.12.0)).
    - **CLAIMED** caveat from a maintainer's own reply on #322: adapter *mis-routing* affected three separate reports and "all arrive over a proxy", because the `Advert:` diagnostic line is emitted only by the native BlueZ handler — a proxy-specific gap, acknowledged rather than denied.
13. **Gadgetbridge: no.** — **CLAIMED**: its scales section lists Xiaomi only, with the Body Composition Scale 2 marked "poorly supported" ([gadgetbridge.org/gadgets/scales](https://gadgetbridge.org/gadgets/scales/)). The only recent scale work found is storing raw impedance for the **Xiaomi** Mi Body Composition Scale ([Codeberg PR #6683](https://codeberg.org/Freeyourgadget/Gadgetbridge/pulls/6683), [#6694](https://codeberg.org/Freeyourgadget/Gadgetbridge/pulls/6694)). No Fitdays/Icomon/OKOK handler surfaced.
14. **Theengs / OpenMQTTGateway: no decoder for this family.** — **CLAIMED**: the request is an open issue ([#503](https://github.com/theengs/decoder/issues/503)); the maintainer's stock answer for scales is "if your scale broadcasts its details through BLE advertising, yes, then it will be possible to integrate a new decoder" ([community thread](https://community.openmqttgateway.com/t/integration-for-bathroom-scales-possible/3145)). Note the asymmetry: `ble-scale-sync` now *uses* ESPHome/HA advertisements itself, so OpenMQTTGateway is not required to reuse that hardware.
15. **Home Assistant has a working Fitdays integration — cloud-side, not BLE.** — **MEASURED**: `AboveColin/HA-Fitdays` builds one HA device per member profile with weight, BMI, body fat (%, kg), muscle (%, kg), skeletal muscle, body water, bone, protein, visceral fat, BMR, body age, impedance, subcutaneous fat, heart rate and target weight, and imports 400 days into long-term statistics. Licence **MIT**, **3 stars**, `pushed_at 2026-09-15`, not archived. It is a read-only cloud poller ([README](https://github.com/AboveColin/HA-Fitdays)).
16. **The ICOMON ecosystem has a second, unrelated scale client — kitchen scales.** — **CLAIMED**: `pyfitdaysplus` is an async Python BLE client for the ICOMON/Fitdays+ **kitchen** scale KG2458ULB-D (BLE name `MY_SCALE`, "protocol 113 GeneralV2"), covering weight/tare/unit and voice food selections — not body composition ([PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/pyfitdaysplus/)). Worth knowing it exists, because food/diet logging is in garsync's stated scope; do not confuse it with the bathroom scale.

### 4. The cloud route — protocol, clients, and its real shape

17. **The Fitdays cloud is an undocumented, signed, read-only-by-convention JSON API, and its protocol is now public in two languages.** — **MEASURED** in source:
    - Hosts: `https://online-eu.fitdays.cn/api` (default), `https://online.fitdays.cn/api` (cn), `https://online-us.fitdays.cn/api` (us); `app_ver "1.27.6"`; `country` default `NL`; `device_model` is not validated; `User-Agent okhttp/4.9.1`; success `code == 0`; auth-error codes `{401, 1002, 1003, 10001}`; region redirect is `code == 302` carrying a `domain`; default history window **400 days** ([constants.py](https://github.com/AboveColin/fitdays/blob/main/fitdays/constants.py)).
    - Signing: `sign = md5_lower(quote_plus("&".join(f"{k}={v}" for k in sorted(params)) + "hxsign"))`, with `capp_ver` appended **after** signing; password sent as `md5_upper(md5_upper(password + "hx"))` ([signing.py](https://github.com/AboveColin/fitdays/blob/main/fitdays/signing.py)).
    - Endpoints: `POST users/login`, `POST sync/syncFromServer` (note the inversion: `start_time` is *now*, `end_time` is the older bound), `POST sync/syncIncrements` with `sync_time` for deltas ([client.py](https://github.com/AboveColin/fitdays/blob/main/fitdays/client.py)).
    - Fields (raw keys, **MEASURED** in `models.py`): `weight_g` (the integer the scale actually sent — prefer over `weight_kg`, which is a lossy float round-trip such as 62.499998092651), `bfr` body fat, `sfr` subcutaneous, `uvi` visceral, `rom` muscle, `rosm` skeletal muscle, `bm` bone mass kg, `vwc` body water, `pp` protein, `bmr`, `bodyage`, `hr`, **`adc` = impedance**, `electrode`; `ext_data` is a JSON *string* carrying `height`; `devices[]` carries `mac`, `sn`, `model`, `firmware_ver`, plus `ext_data.measureHeart` and **`ext_data.offlineMeasure`** ([models.py](https://github.com/AboveColin/fitdays/blob/main/fitdays/models.py)).
18. **A second, independent TypeScript implementation of the same API exists.** — **MEASURED**: `roquerodrigo/fitdays-api` (MIT, **3 stars**, created 2026-05-02, `pushed_at 2026-09-22`) exposes `login`, `loginWithPhone`, `syncFromServer({startTime, endTime})`, `syncAll()` and a raw `request<T>()`, with region hosts `us|eu|cn` and the same `startTime`-is-the-upper-bound quirk ([README](https://github.com/roquerodrigo/fitdays-api), GitHub API). An MCP server and a Cloudflare-Worker MCP fork built on it exist (**CLAIMED**, from package listings).
19. **The official app vendor confirms 13 body parameters and four health platforms — none of them Garmin or Strava.** — **MEASURED** on ICOMON's own FAQ: 13 parameters = weight, BMI, body fat rate, fat-free body weight, subcutaneous fat, visceral fat, body water, skeletal muscle rate, muscle mass, bone mass, protein rate, BMR, body age; and "Sync with Apple Health, Google fit, Fitbit, Samsung Health" ([online.fitdays.cn FAQ](https://online.fitdays.cn/app/faq?language=en)). Garmin is named nowhere; the Garmin forum answer is a chain through Fitbit → MyFitnessPal → Garmin Connect (**CLAIMED**, [Garmin forums](https://forums.garmin.com/apps-software/mobile-apps-web/f/connect-iq-store-android/405102/can-i-connect-my-fitdays-scale-to-the-garmin-app)).
20. **CSV export exists and a third party already parses it.** — **MEASURED** (the script) / **CLAIMED** (the export UI): `uploadBodyComposition.sh` reads `fitdays.csv`, splits columns 1,2,4,9,11,12 as datetime, weight, body fat, water, muscle, bone, converts bone kg → percentage of weight, and POSTs to `https://runalyze.com/api/v1/metrics/bodyComposition` ([GitLab](https://gitlab.com/leroyzwakman1/runalyze-fitdays)). The CSV column layout and its "15:35 22/01/2022" timestamp format are visible in the script. Licence not stated in the repository tree I listed (3 files: README, `.gitignore`, the script).
21. **Wi-Fi models that upload without the phone: claimed only by a third-party doc site, unverified by the vendor.** — The claim appears on `fitdays.org` ("some scales are Wi-Fi & Bluetooth dual-mode… data is synced to the cloud over Wi-Fi so it is available whenever you open the app — even when your phone is not nearby", [connect-the-fitdays-app](https://fitdays.org/docs/user-manual/connect-the-fitdays-app)). I mark this **CLAIMED / provenance unverified**: `fitdays.org` carries no copyright, ownership, disclaimer or `fitdays.app` reference I could find, while ICOMON's own FAQ never mentions Wi-Fi scales. **Do not design around Wi-Fi-without-phone until you can test it on your own unit.**
22. **Offline buffering almost certainly exists for some models — and the cloud exposes the capability flag.** — **MEASURED** that the flag exists (`ScaleDevice.offline_measure` ← `ext_data.offlineMeasure`, [models.py](https://github.com/AboveColin/fitdays/blob/main/fitdays/models.py)); **CLAIMED** that the behaviour is "weigh without the phone, results stored in the scale's memory, upload on next app open (many models store up to 8 users / 40 records)" ([fitdays.org](https://fitdays.org/docs/user-manual/how-to-measure)). **INFERRED**: since the app must be open and connected to receive a measurement in the official flow ("Keep the App on home page or click Measure", [FAQ](https://online.fitdays.cn/app/faq?language=en)), the cloud route's latency is either "seconds while the phone is in the room with the app open" or "at the next app open" — never a scale-originated upload, unless the unverified Wi-Fi claim holds.
23. **The cloud is poll-only.** — **MEASURED**: both client libraries and the HA integration fetch on a timer; HA uses `SCAN_INTERVAL = timedelta(minutes=30)` and `HISTORY_DAYS = 400`, with the comment "scales are weighed at most a handful of times a day, so there is nothing to gain from polling hard" ([HA-Fitdays `__init__.py`](https://github.com/AboveColin/HA-Fitdays/blob/main/custom_components/fitdays/__init__.py), [`const.py`](https://github.com/AboveColin/HA-Fitdays/blob/main/custom_components/fitdays/const.py)). There is no webhook, no MQTT, no push. Polling harder is your only latency lever (`syncIncrements` exists for deltas).
24. **Every weight-only reading is flagged, and the vendor's own flow explains when it happens.** — **MEASURED**: the Python client exposes `is_weight_only` so a caller can "skip the body-composition fields rather than charting nulls", and treats `0` as "not measured" for optional metrics ([README](https://github.com/AboveColin/fitdays), [models.py](https://github.com/AboveColin/fitdays/blob/main/fitdays/models.py)); the vendor FAQ states that stepping on "with socks, only weight and BMI will be measured", and lists bare, dry feet and an open, connected app as prerequisites for body data ([FAQ](https://online.fitdays.cn/app/faq?language=en)). For garsync this is a data-quality gate, not a nicety: a large share of rows will legitimately have no composition, and the ingest must distinguish "not measured" from "failed to parse".
25. **The cloud route has a narrow, enumerable break surface — and it is entirely in the app's private client.** — **MEASURED** as a list of the exact values a Fitdays app update can invalidate: the two salts (`hxsign` for `sign`, `hx` for the password digest), `app_ver "1.27.6"`, the three regional hosts, the `code 302` redirect contract, the auth-error code set, the `syncFromServer` parameter inversion, and the `ext_data` JSON-string encoding. None of these are documented by the vendor; a client break is silent until a fetch returns `code != 0`, which is why the poller must alert on auth/API errors rather than retrying forever.

### 5. Hardware route and real-time answers

26. **A passive listener works only for broadcast families; a GATT connect needs a real central.** — **MEASURED**: HA's advertisement stream "exposes advertisements over this API, not GATT connections. Only scales whose reading is in the advertisement" work over it; GATT-only scales need the ESP32 proxy (which relays connect/write/read over MQTT) or a host with a Bluetooth adapter ([ha-bluetooth](https://blescalesync.dev/guide/ha-bluetooth), [esp32-proxy](https://blescalesync.dev/guide/esp32-proxy)). A Pi Zero 2W is the recommended cheap node for the native-transport case (**CLAIMED**, [FAQ](https://blescalesync.dev/faq)).
27. **Real-time push is achievable on the BLE routes and impossible on the cloud route.** — **INFERRED** from MEASURED mechanics: broadcast families emit the final frame the instant the reading stabilises; the `FFB0` families deliver notify frames during the session; the cloud only ever sees the app's upload. An always-on listener therefore gets **near-real-time push** for free; the cloud gets you a **poll-bounded** copy.
28. **Replacing the scale is a legitimate engineering baseline.** — **MEASURED**: `Renpho ES-CS20M / ES-32MD / Elis 1 / FITINDEX / Sencor (QN-Scale)` is described as "the most common protocol; many rebrands" and is supported for body composition in `ble-scale-sync`, and the Mi Scale 2 needs no pairing on any transport ([supported scales](https://blescalesync.dev/guide/supported-scales)). If the current unit turns out to be a weight-only or obstruction-heavy family, that is the cheapest robustness purchase available.

## Route comparison

| # | Route | Requires | Phone in room? | Breaks if the vendor changes the app? | Payload | Real-time? | Realistic cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Passive BLE** (broadcast families: AAA002/007/013, OKOK/Chipsea) | Always-on host with a BLE adapter or ESP32/ESPHome proxy; advertisement decoder | No | No (firmware-only risk) | Weight always; impedance in the stream, but the 0xA6 frame is undecoded upstream → local BIA needed | **Yes**, push on stabilisation | Low–medium: reuse openScale's decoder logic or `ble-scale-sync`'s; write the 0xA6 path yourself |
| 2 | **Active GATT daemon** (`FFB0/FFB1/FFB2` SWAN/Icomon/Lefu class) | BLE-capable always-on host in range; connect-and-hold daemon | No (but the Fitdays app must not already hold the link) | No | Best local option: composite frames carry fat/muscle/bone/water, or weight+impedance → full local BIA | **Yes**, at the moment of weighing | Medium: `ble-scale-sync` supports this family today; a bespoke daemon is ~1 week of work + captures |
| 3 | **Fitdays cloud poll** | Fitdays account; Python client; SOPS-stored credential; polling scheduler | **Yes** — the app must be open and connected (or the flag-gated offline buffer must exist) | **Yes** — app version, salts and endpoints are all private and rotate with the app | **Richest**: 13 vendor-computed parameters incl. visceral fat, body age, BMR, subcutaneous fat, + heart rate and impedance, 400-day backfill | No — poll-bounded (≥ minutes) | Low to build, ongoing fragility; two MIT clients exist |
| 4 | **App-mediated export** (CSV / Apple Health / Google Fit / Fitbit / Samsung Health) | Manual export + a script (the Runalyze script is a template); or a phone-side bridge for Health apps | Yes | Yes | Whatever the export carries (the CSV path seen carries weight, body fat, water, muscle, bone) | No | Low to script, high to keep running; manual forever |
| 5 | **Replace the scale** (QN-Scale / Renpho / Mi class) | Money | No | No | Body composition, best-supported protocols in both openScale and `ble-scale-sync` | Yes | One purchase |

**Ranking by robustness**: 1 ≈ 2 ≈ 5 (no vendor in the loop) > 3 (single point of failure is a private API) > 4 (manual, or phone-side code you do not control).
**Ranking by payload**: 3 > 2 > 1 > 4 (and 4 ≈ 3 only for the columns the export happens to include).
**Recommended composition**: **2 (or 1) as primary + 3 as backfill**, reconciled on (profile, timestamp, weight). Path 4 as an emergency export only.

## What this means for garsync

**Do not pick a route before you know which family your scale is in — that is a 10-minute test, and it collapses the decision.** The identification procedure, in order:

1. Put the phone in airplane mode or take it out of range; close the Fitdays app; power-cycle the scale and step on it once.
2. Scan for BLE advertisements from a Linux host with `ble-scale-sync scan` (Docker, `--network host`) or `bluetoothctl`, or from nRF Connect on a phone that is *not* running Fitdays.
3. Record per nearby device: advertised **name**, advertised **service UUIDs**, and **manufacturer company ID + raw bytes**.
4. Read that against findings 4, 6 and 7:

- name `AAA002`/`AAA007`/`AAA013`, or an unnamed device with manufacturer-id low byte `0xC0` whose 6-byte payload passes the `& 0x1F` checksum → **Route 1**, passive.
- name contains `SWAN` / `icomon` / `YG`, or the device advertises service `0xFFB0` → **Route 2**, GATT; the composite frames may hand you the scale's own fat/muscle/bone/water with no BIA maths of your own.
- name `ADV` / `Chipsea-BLE` / `Yoda*` with manufacturer id `0x20CA` → **Route 1 with impedance included** (V20), the best passive case.

**Opinionated architecture.** Keep the two concerns apart, because they fail differently and one of them is a legal boundary:

1. **Capture as a standalone process, not as a library inside `src/garsync/`.** `ble-scale-sync` is **GPL-3.0** and garsync is a portfolio repo; the same function is available by running it as its own container and consuming its **MQTT** or **Webhook** exporter — that keeps the copyleft at a process boundary and lands naturally on kubelab's existing MQTT/Redis/Grafana stack. If you would rather own the code, `bleak` + an `FFB0` implementation modelled on `MGBHandler.kt` is small and unencumbered, and the frame layouts above are enough to write it. Either choice **adds a dependency**, which under this repo's Discipline Gate means a spec, not a shortcut.
2. **Cloud poller inside garsync, as a normal `src/garsync/` module with its own repository binding.** `fitdays` (PyPI, MIT) is a clean async client in the same language as garsync, and the field map in finding 17 is the full list of things you can close the loop on: `adc` (impedance), `bm`/`rom`/`rosm`/`vwc`/`pp`/`uvi`/`sfr`/`bfr`, `bmr`, `bodyage`, `hr`, plus `weight_g` as the lossless integer. Persist the *raw* payload too — the client keeps `.raw` on every model for exactly this reason, and garsync is a data-warehouse-shaped project that will regret discarding `ext_data`.
3. **Reconciliation has to be explicit, because the two paths will not agree.** Local BIA over raw impedance and the vendor's cloud figures are different models: the `ble-scale-sync` contributor measured **39.7 %** body fat from one formula versus **29.1 %** from openScale's `StandardImpedanceLib` on the same reading (103.3 kg / 518 Ω / 193 cm / male 55) ([#322](https://github.com/KristianP26/ble-scale-sync/issues/322)). Pick one source of truth for the body-composition series — I would pick the vendor's, because it is what the app shows Manu and what any future clinician-facing export will contain — and store the locally-derived values in separate columns, flagged by algorithm, rather than merged into the same field.
4. **Near real time means two different latencies, and the UI should say which.** BLE capture is seconds after the reading regardless of the phone; cloud is bounded by your poll interval **and** by whether the app was open. The `offlineMeasure` flag on the device record is the thing to surface — a weigh-in that never reaches the cloud is invisible to a poller, and the failure is silent by default.
5. **Do not build the Garmin mirror twice.** garsync already pins `garminconnect`; that library has `add_body_composition(timestamp, weight, percent_fat, percent_hydration, visceral_fat_mass, bone_mass, muscle_mass, basal_met, active_met, physique_rating, metabolic_age, visceral_fat_rating, bmi)`, which assembles a FIT file and uploads it (**MEASURED** in master source). So "close the loop on body composition" in the Garmin direction is a call garsync can already make, with no new dependency — but **verify the pinned range `>=0.3.11,<0.4.0` exposes that signature**; I checked master, not the pinned tag, and this is exactly the kind of thing that changes across a minor.
6. **Credentials.** The cloud client needs the Fitdays email plus the double-MD5 digest, and the digest alone re-authenticates — it is password-equivalent for this API. That belongs in the existing SOPS+age path like every other secret in this repo, never in `config.yaml`, never in a log line, and never in a research artifact.

**What I would actually do, in order:** (1) run the identification scan and, if the family is broadcast, capture 60 seconds of raw advertisements and confirm weight + a plausible impedance frame arrive; (2) stand up the cloud poller first, because it is low-cost, MIT, and delivers 400 days of history immediately — that makes the dashboard useful this week; (3) add the BLE path second, as a container, once the family is known, because that is the real-time and vendor-independent half; (4) only then touch the BLE→local-BIA maths, and only with a validated ohm reading.

## Open questions and decisions needed

1. **Which family is the actual scale?** Unanswerable from any source — it needs the advertisement scan above (name, service UUIDs, manufacturer ID, raw bytes). Everything downstream branches on it. **Your input required.**
2. **Is the impedance (`adc`) field populated on your account today?** Check one measurement in the Fitdays app against the cloud response. If not, the whole BLE-BIA sub-project is moot and only weight is at stake.
3. **Does your model buffer offline measurements, and does the app upload them later?** The `offlineMeasure` flag exists in the device record (**MEASURED**); the behaviour does not. Test: weigh with the phone in another room, then open the app and see whether the record appears with the original timestamp. This single test determines whether Route 3 needs the phone present at all.
4. **Are there Wi-Fi Fitdays models, and would the scale upload without any app?** Only claimed by a third-party doc site with no verifiable ownership. If true, and your model is one of them, the cloud route becomes phone-free and jumps in value. Unverified.
5. **Decision: GPL-3.0 by process boundary, or your own `bleak` daemon?** This is a licence/ownership call, not a technical one, and it changes the dependency footprint either way.
6. **Decision: is Garmin the system of record for body composition, or is garsync's own SQLite?** Both are viable; doing both means reconciliation rules you have to write and defend.
7. **Google Fit as an intermediate hop is a decaying bet.** The Fitdays app syncs to Google Fit (**MEASURED**, vendor FAQ), but Google's Fit APIs are on a deprecation path in favour of Health Connect — I did **not** verify the current deprecation timeline in this session and deliberately state no date. Do not build the primary path on it.
8. **Do not assume `0xFFF0`.** If you start a reverse-engineering effort, start from `0xFFB0` for the connectable class and from manufacturer data for the broadcast class; the FFF0 trio belongs to other families in the sources read here.

## Gaps

- **No public HCI snoop of a Fitdays session for the `0xA6` impedance frame in the broadcast family.** The maintainer's PR is the only artifact, it is unmerged, and its own description discloses AI assistance in deriving the cipher. Treat the `0xA0AC` impedance decode as unproven until you capture it yourself.
- **No verified list of which retail models ship which family.** Names are the only reliable signal, and names are the OEM's to choose — the same model number can ship with different internals (documented for the Renpho ES-CS20M, where "some units use broadcast-only firmware that does not allow GATT connections").
- **The Wi-Fi-without-phone claim is unverifiable from vendor-owned material** in this session.
- **No statement anywhere on Fitdays' terms of service** regarding polling a reverse-engineered private API. I did not fetch the ToS — worth reading before making the cloud poller a permanent dependency, since this is a portfolio-visible repo.
- **`garminconnect` pinned-version capability is unverified**: I read master, garsync pins `>=0.3.11,<0.4.0`. Verify `add_body_composition` at the pinned tag.
